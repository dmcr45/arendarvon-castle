=== UNLOCK on something that is not a door (the CURTAIN, passage 21)
> UNLOCK CURTAIN
  YOU CAN'T UNLOCK A CURTAIN.
=== UNLOCK a locked door, then look at it (door 4, CORRIDOR 20 -> GREAT HALL)
> EXAMINE DOOR
  YOU EXAMINE THE DOOR AHEAD. IT IS LOCKED.
> UNLOCK DOOR
  YOU UNLOCK THE DOOR.
> EXAMINE DOOR
  YOU EXAMINE THE DOOR AHEAD. IT IS CLOSED BUT NOT LOCKED.
> OPEN DOOR
  YOU OPEN THE DOOR.
=== LOCK an open door (door 5, SHORT CORRIDOR 24 -> COURTYARD)
> EXAMINE DOOR
  YOU EXAMINE THE DOOR AHEAD. IT IS OPEN.
> LOCK DOOR
  YOU CAN'T LOCK AN OPEN DOOR.
> EXAMINE DOOR
  YOU EXAMINE THE DOOR AHEAD. IT IS OPEN.
> GO AHEAD
  YOU GO AHEAD. YOU GO PAST THE DOOR. YOU ARE IN THE COURTYARD. THE GIRL ENTERS FROM THE ENTRY-HALL TO YOUR LEFT.
=== LOCK then UNLOCK a NAILED door (door 34, LONG CORRIDOR 93 -> PRIVATE-ROOM)
> EXAMINE DOOR
  YOU EXAMINE THE DOOR AHEAD. IT IS NAILED SHUT.
> GO AHEAD
  YOU GO AHEAD. YOU BUMP INTO THE DOOR.
> LOCK DOOR
  IT IS NAILED SHUT.
> UNLOCK DOOR
  THE DOOR IS NOT LOCKED.
> EXAMINE DOOR
  YOU EXAMINE THE DOOR AHEAD. IT IS NAILED SHUT.
> GO AHEAD
  YOU GO AHEAD. YOU BUMP INTO THE DOOR.
